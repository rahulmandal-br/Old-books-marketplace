About BookLoop

Our Mission

BookLoop ek simple, eco-friendly platform hai jo college students ko purani textbooks aur reference books aaps me buy aur sell karne me help karta hai. Isse students ke paise bhi bachte hain aur paper waste bhi nahi hota.

How It Works

1. List Your Book: Apni purani book ki photo aur details 'Sell a Book' page par dalein.
2. Set Price: Apne hisab se reasonable rate set karein.
3. Get Connected: Jo student aapki book kharidna chahta hai, vo direct aapko contact karega.
